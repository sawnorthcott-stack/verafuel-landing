Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

VeraFuel ("we", "the app") is built and operated by Simon Northcott. This policy explains what data VeraFuel collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

VeraFuel records the food, training, sleep, and body data you choose to log: meals, macros and micronutrients, workouts, sleep hours, weight and body composition, and progress photos if you add them. Workouts, sleep and weight can also arrive from Apple Health if you connect it, which VeraFuel reads only, it never writes back. We also store your account key if you create an account to sync your data across devices.

Why we collect it

Solely to calculate your nutrition targets, show your trends, and power the coaching features you use inside the app. We don't collect anything beyond what the app's features need.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored securely via Supabase (our backend provider) and associated only with your device or account key, never linked to other users' data. If you don't create an account, your data stays on your device.

Backups and recovery

So that a sync fault cannot wipe your history, our server keeps a copy of your previous data before it is overwritten. These copies never include your photos. A routine copy is kept for 14 days. If a save ever arrives holding far less than the one it replaces, which is what a failure looks like, that copy is kept for 90 days so it can be restored. The app cannot read these copies, only we can, and only to put your own data back.

One consequence worth knowing. If you use "Reset all data" in Settings, one recovery copy of what you deleted can remain for up to 90 days before it expires. If you would rather it went immediately, email us and we will remove it the same day.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. If you use "Share with my coach," your coach sees only what that feature explicitly shows (food, workouts, weight) for as long as you keep the connection active, and you can revoke it at any time, instantly.

Third-party services

Barcode lookups use Open Food Facts, a public food database. No personal data is sent with a lookup, only the scanned barcode.

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat, which records an anonymous customer ID and your purchase history so the app knows what you have access to. It never receives your food, training or health data. Payment itself is taken by Apple, and we never see your card details.

Your control

You can export or delete your data at any time from Settings. Deleting your account removes your data from our servers, and the recovery copies described above go with it in the same step.

Children

VeraFuel is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.
