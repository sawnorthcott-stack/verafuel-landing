The 7-Day
Fuel Check

Seven days of logging, one honest read.

Calories are the easy part. The things that quietly catch plant-based runners out are the ones you only find out about months later, in a blood test or in a race that falls apart at mile 18.

So I built a finite version of the question. Log seven days of normal eating, then get a plain-English read on the five that plant-based eating makes hardest.

FeIron B12Vitamin B12 ALAOmega-3 IIodine LeuProtein quality

What you actually get

For each of the five: your real average against your target, a verdict in ordinary words rather than a traffic light, and one specific food that closes the gap.

Protein quality is the one most people have never seen measured. It is not how much protein you ate, it is whether enough of it arrived in one sitting to be useful, which on a plant-based diet is a different question with a different answer.

How it works, and what it refuses to do

Seven logged days, not seven calendar days. Miss a Tuesday and nothing breaks. A streak that punishes you for having a life is not a measurement, it is a guilt trip.

No badge at the end. No confetti, no points, no shareable trophy. The reward is a report worth reading.

Start whenever. There is no cohort and no start gun. Day one is whenever you log day one.
