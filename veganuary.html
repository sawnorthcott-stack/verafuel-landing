Where January usually goes wrong

Most people who give up on Veganuary do it in the third week, and almost all of them say the same thing. They just felt tired.

It is rarely the diet. Plant iron is harder to absorb than the version in meat, and a cup of tea with your meal will undo a good chunk of what you did manage. There is no reliable plant source of B12, so that one needs a decision rather than good intentions. And plant foods carry more volume for the same energy, so you eat the plate you have always eaten, feel perfectly full, and come up short anyway. Do that for three weeks and you will feel exactly as rough as you would expect to.

None of that is an argument against eating this way. I have done it for years. It is an argument for knowing your numbers for the first month instead of guessing, and then not having to think about it again.
