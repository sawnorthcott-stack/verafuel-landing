Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is VeraFuel only for vegan athletes?

VeraFuel is built around the needs of plant-based athletes, so it suits anyone who eats vegan, vegetarian or mostly plant-based. It is easy to adapt if your diet changes.

How much does VeraFuel cost?

VeraFuel is free to download and free for 14 days. After that you choose a monthly contribution of £1.99, £3.99 or £6.99. Every option unlocks the full app, with no locked features and no premium tiers.

How is VeraFuel different from a calorie counter?

A calorie counter records numbers. VeraFuel interprets them. It connects your training, nutrition and recovery, and coaches you on what the data means for plant-based fuelling, including protein, iron, recovery and energy availability, in a calm voice that never guilts you.

Does VeraFuel connect to Apple Health?

Yes. On iPhone, VeraFuel can read sleep, weight and workouts from Apple Health, including data from watches and trackers that sync into it, so you do not have to enter them twice. Nothing is written back to Apple Health.

How does VeraFuel help with iron on a plant-based diet?

VeraFuel estimates how much iron you actually absorb, not just how much you eat, and shows it against your daily target so you can spot gaps early. It is guidance rather than a diagnosis, so if you are worried about your iron levels, speak to your GP.

Is VeraFuel medical advice?

No. VeraFuel is sports nutrition coaching and education, not medical advice or a diagnosis. Where it notices a pattern such as sustained under-fuelling, it says so gently and points you to your GP.

Who created VeraFuel?

VeraFuel was created by Simon Northcott, a sports nutrition coach with an MSc in Food Science and a Level 4 Elite Sports Nutrition qualification. He has been plant-based for nine years and trains six days a week.

Is there an Android version?

VeraFuel is currently available for iPhone only, on the App Store.
